Hi, I am Yağmur!

Hi, I'm the person behind Avalon Chronicles. I'm 21 and a university student. Film, literature, and nature have always been huge parts of my life. I've been scribbling something or other for as long as I can remember. It started with poetry, and over time it turned into however I express myself about any kind of art that makes me feel something and lets me find a piece of myself in it. Even though I have a lot of answers to this, my favorite film is Little Miss Sunshine. My favorite shows are Merlin and Game of Thrones — yes, I couldn't choose. My favorite author is Jane Austen, and my favorite song is "Pictures of You" by The Cure. I love gardening, watching documentaries, autumn, unfinished stories, and taking photographs. Beyond that, I'm drawn to where cognitive science meets storytelling. I like thinking about how a silence or a repeated pattern can shape what we feel before we've even noticed it. Watching people often makes me feel like I'm part of a story within the life I'm living.Merhaba, Avalon Chronicles'ın arkasındaki kişi benim. 21 yaşındayım ve üniversite öğrencisiyim. Avalon Chronicles is where all of this comes together. Here I write about the films I watch, the books I read, and the feelings they leave behind. Sometimes it's just a tiny memory that makes me pick up a pen, a thought that won't leave my mind, or an image that stays with me for a long time.Avalon Chronicles ise bütün bunların bir araya geldiği yer.
